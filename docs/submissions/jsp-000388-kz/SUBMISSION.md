# JSP-000388: request for review of a known quadratic-family formalization

## English

Please assess the included Lean formalization as a contribution concerning
JSP-000388 / Erdos 477. It proves that no integer polynomial
`a*x^2+b*x+c` with `a != 0` and `a|b` admits a unique additive complement in
the integers. All coefficients, all integer inputs, and arbitrary candidate
complement sets are quantified. The proof includes `b=0`, both signs of `a`,
and a Mathlib polynomial-evaluation endpoint.

This is a **partial-family contribution**, not a solution of the original
general existence question. That question has a known positive answer for
some higher even powers. Mathematical discovery credit remains with the
historical authors and provers identified in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).
We request assessment of incremental formalization, not new mathematical
discovery or global first priority.
