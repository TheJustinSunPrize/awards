# References and reuse boundary

## Mathematics

1. **P. Erdős and A. Hajnal**, *On chromatic graphs*, Mat. Lapok (1967), 1–4. The question and this reference are recorded on the [Erdős 922 problem page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/latex/922).
2. **Jon H. Folkman**, *An upper bound on the chromatic number of a graph*, in *Combinatorial theory and its application, II* (Proc. Colloq., Balatonfüred, 1969), North-Holland, Amsterdam, 1970, pp. 437–457. [Publisher/society contents](https://www.bolyai.hu/files/kotetek_4_1970.pdf); [bibliographic record and theorem in the later research paper, reference Fol70](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.11429v2). The finite mathematical theorem is Folkman's, not the submitter's.
3. **Marthe Bonamy, Pierre Charbit, Oscar Defrain, Gwenaël Joret, Aurélie Lagoutte, Vincent Limouzy, Lucas Pastor, and Jean-Sébastien Sereni**, *Revisiting a theorem by Folkman on graph colouring*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics **27**(1), P1.56 (2020). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.37236/8899); [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11429v2). This is a modern proof and exposition of the finite result, including the minimal-counterexample, critical-splitting, even-cycle, and diamond arguments. It is included as mathematical background, not as a claim that this package contributes a new finite proof.
4. **N. G. de Bruijn and Paul Erdős**, *A colour problem for infinite graphs and a problem in the theory of relations*, Proceedings of the Section of Sciences of the Koninklijke Nederlandse Akademie van Wetenschappen, Series A **54** (1951), 371–373. [Institutional record and original paper](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/a-colour-problem-for-infinite-graphs-and-a-problem-in-the-theory-/). The passage from all finite induced subgraphs to the whole graph is this classical compactness principle.
