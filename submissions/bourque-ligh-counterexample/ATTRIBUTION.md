# Attribution and sources

Keith Bourque and Steve Ligh posed the conjecture in 1992.
Pentti Haukkanen, Jun Wang and Juha Sillanpää published the nine-element
counterexample in 1997. Mathematical discovery and resolution are credited to
these original authors in their respective roles.

This contribution supplies Lean definitions, a machine-checked kernel
certificate, the determinant argument, the real-number bridge, and
reproduction materials. It is submitted for recognition of formalization.
The proposed formalization recipient is `RECIPIENT-BOURQUE-LIGH-A`;
confirmation is pending.

## Original conjecture

K. Bourque and S. Ligh, *On GCD and LCM matrices*,
Linear Algebra and its Applications **174** (1992), 65–74.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(92)90042-9).

## Published counterexample

P. Haukkanen, J. Wang and J. Sillanpää, *On Smith's determinant*,
Linear Algebra and its Applications **258** (1997), 251–269.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0024-3795(96)00192-9).

The same counterexample and its attribution are reproduced in Section 6.4 of
I. Korkee, M. Mattila and P. Haukkanen,
*A lattice-theoretic approach to the Bourque–Ligh conjecture*.
[Public manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.5428).
