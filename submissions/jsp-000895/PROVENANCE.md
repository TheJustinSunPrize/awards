# Mathematical source, definitions, and duplicate review

The known upper-bound argument is due to Stefan Glock, *Triple systems with no three triples spanning at most five points*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 51 (2019), 230–236, [DOI 10.1112/blms.12224](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12224), [arXiv:1809.02100](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02100), [Section 2.1, Lemma 4 and its proof](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.02100#S2.SS1). Lemma 4 states the `n²/5` bound, and the proof's final count yields the finite sharpening `n(n-1)/5` formalized here. The paper's mathematical ideas are cited; its copyrighted text is not redistributed or relicensed.

The definitions are from [plby/lean-proofs at 8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e, Erdos1076.lean](https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs/blob/8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e/src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos1076.lean). The fixed original file identity and reused declaration list are recorded in [source-origin.json](source-origin.json). Source-level comparison confirmed identical bodies after whitespace normalization for all six definitions; this is not a claim to have compiled an equivalence against the whole lower-bound module.

That source explicitly states Apache 2.0, Copyright (c) 2026 Boris Alexeev, and identifies Boris Alexeev and Codex in its author notice, with Codex and GPT-5.6 Sol also listed as formal authors. Those notices and the modification are retained in this package. The new upper-bound proof is prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance. See [NOTICE](NOTICE) and [LICENSE](LICENSE). Attribution is provenance, not another author's endorsement or a recipient nomination.

On 2026-09-17 (Asia/Shanghai), bounded public review examined the award repository's complete 150-PR snapshot and targeted current PR, code and web queries for the problem, `FkFree`, `extremalNumber_five`, the upper constant, codegrees and the new terminal formulas. No complete duplicate of the single-`FkFree 5` finite upper theorem was identified in the checked sources. This is not an exhaustive global absence assertion.

[PR90](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/pull/90) was refreshed and its actual fixed-head source was read at `983a20fcb5f44fa72c3c279e065546a14640784f`. `JSP895/Bridge.lean` defines `FamilyFree` by requiring all `FkFree j` from four through `k`; its `three_mul_card_le_choose` assumes `FkFree 4`, and the extremal upper bound invokes the `j=4` case. Its assembly therefore does not cover only `FkFree 5`. The pinned plby main source supplies a different lower construction and a disproof of the literal universal `1/6` assertion, not this upper theorem.

The source and scope review was performed by separate AI agents, not organizer-appointed verifiers or human referees. Local compilation and replay are reported separately from semantic review, and neither is a prize decision.

## F5-and-F6 extension

[Glock, Section 3, Theorem 8 and its proof](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.02100#S3) gives the combined-family finite upper bound `3|G| <= choose(n,2)` and a separate lower bound yielding the asymptotic. This package formalizes the finite upper part only. The supporting finite statement also appears in the historical problem discussion; no assertion is made that Glock first discovered it.

Additional bounded review checked the two actual `erdos_1076.variants.supporting_theorem` declarations in [ryantuck/erdos-ai, fixed commit f32b288](https://github.com/ryantuck/erdos-ai/tree/f32b2886f8f005d481533036d0af4fe36e8e48f7), under `deepmind/deepmind/1076.lean` and `deepmind/deepmind-v2/1076.lean`. Both terminate in `sorry`; they establish an existing statement, not a complete duplicate proof. Other checked Lean sources provided definitions or axioms. A refreshed complete 162-PR award snapshot identified PR90 as the relevant other submission, still at the distinct F4-based scope. A full untruncated tree at the fixed plby commit contained only the main 1076 Lean file and its comparator, with no sibling upper proof. This is bounded duplicate checking, not global absence or priority certification.
