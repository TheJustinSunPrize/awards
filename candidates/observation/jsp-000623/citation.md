# JSP-000623: Cochromatic growth on orientable surfaces

## English

John Gimbel and Carsten Thomassen proved that the maximum cochromatic number of
a finite graph embeddable on the orientable surface of genus g has order
sqrt(g)/log(g). This submission completes the Lean formalization for the ordinary
rotation-system embedding class, including disconnected graphs and isolated
vertices. The new deletion proof derives the induced-subgraph heredity needed
by the existing cochromatic estimates.

The [complete Lean project](../../../proofs/jsp-000623/README.md) and
[fixed-source evidence guide](../../../proofs/jsp-000623/SUBMISSION.md) contain
the exact statements, source attribution, pinned dependencies, reproducible
verification commands, source archive, and checksums. A dedicated Lean workflow
rebuilds the committed project, checks final theorem types and axioms, and
replays all project modules with the Lean kernel.

The candidate is submitted for designated review. Its official verification
record and recipient confirmation remain pending.
