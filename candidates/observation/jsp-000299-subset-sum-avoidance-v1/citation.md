# Candidate observation

## English

This candidate observation is associated with [JSP-000299 — How large can a subset of a finite integer range be if none of its subset sums equals a prescribed target?](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/main/problems/catalog-0201-0300.md#JSP-000299). It publishes `Statement.lean` and `Proof.lean` for a complete, machine-checked Lean formalization of a **complete answer for the case where the prescribed target equals the top of the range**: for `n ≥ 2`, the largest size of a set `A ⊆ {1, …, n}` such that no subset of `A` sums to `n` is exactly `⌊n/2⌋`. The lower bound is the explicit construction `A = {⌈n/2⌉, …, n-1}` (`jsp000299_lower`); the upper bound maps `A` injectively into `{1, …, ⌊n/2⌋}` via `a ↦ min a (n - a)`, a collision forcing two distinct elements `a, b ∈ A` with `a + b = n` (`jsp000299_upper`); `jsp000299` combines both bounds. The proof source is pinned to [jsp-000299-lean](https://github.com/Yaohua-Leo/jsp-000299-lean), with the formalization authored by Yaohua-Leo (AI-assisted via ZCode (GLM)). **Claim boundary: complete theorem for the target-`n` (top of range) case only.** It does not characterize the maximum for a general prescribed target `t`, does not resolve the catalogued open problem (which remains Open), and makes no claim of official JSP verification, award status, recipient identity, or independent review.
