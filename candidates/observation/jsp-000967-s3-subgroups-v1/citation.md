# Candidate observation

## English

This candidate observation is associated with [JSP-000967 — How many subgroups does a symmetric group have, and how are their orders distributed?](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/main/problems/catalog-0901-1000.md#JSP-000967). It publishes `Statement.lean` and `Proof.lean` for a complete, machine-checked Lean formalization of the exact `S₃` instance of the catalog question: the symmetric group `S₃ = Equiv.Perm (Fin 3)` has exactly six subgroups (`jsp000967_count`), and the number of subgroups of order 1, 2, 3, and 6 is respectively 1, 3, 1, and 1 (`jsp000967_orders`), i.e. the full order distribution `{1 ↦ 1, 2 ↦ 3, 3 ↦ 1, 6 ↦ 1}`. The proof source is pinned to [jsp-000967-lean](https://github.com/Yaohua-Leo/jsp-000967-lean), with the formalization authored by Yaohua-Leo (AI-assisted via ZCode (GLM)). **Claim boundary: complete theorem for the S₃ instance only.** It does not address the question for general symmetric groups `Sₙ` with `n ≥ 4`, does not resolve the catalogued open problem (which remains Open), and makes no claim of official JSP verification, award status, recipient identity, or independent review.
