# Candidate observation

## English

This candidate observation is associated with [JSP-000840 — How many triangles must a graph have once its edge count exceeds the maximum bipartite edge count?](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/main/problems/catalog-0801-0900.md#JSP-000840). It publishes `Statement.lean` and `Proof.lean` for the general Rademacher theorem: every finite simple graph with `m > floor(n^2/4)` edges contains at least `floor(n/2)` triangles. **Claim boundary: a complete Lean proof candidate for this stated theorem.** It does not claim the stronger multi-excess-edge bounds discussed in the source; the public source is [the cited Rademacher material](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1964-21.pdf).

A local Lean compilation was run with Lean 4.33.1 and the pinned Mathlib revision. That compiler result is only a reproducibility check; it is not independent verification of statement faithfulness or official JSP verification.

The candidate remains under verification. No award decision, recipient identity, solver or formalizer credit, payment, or official verification is asserted.
