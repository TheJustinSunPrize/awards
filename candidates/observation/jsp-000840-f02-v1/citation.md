# Candidate observation

## English

This preliminary observation is associated with [JSP-000840 — How many triangles must a graph have once its edge count exceeds the maximum bipartite edge count?](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/main/problems/catalog-0801-0900.md#JSP-000840). It publishes `Statement.lean` and `Proof.lean` for the finite specialization that every simple graph on four vertices with five edges contains at least two triangles. This is an `n = 4` specialization, not a formalization or solution of the full catalog problem; the public source is [the cited Rademacher material](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1964-21.pdf).

A local Lean compilation was run with Lean 4.33.1 and the pinned Mathlib revision. That compiler result is only a reproducibility check; it is not independent verification of statement faithfulness or official JSP verification.

The candidate remains under verification. No award decision, recipient identity, solver or formalizer credit, payment, or official verification is asserted.
