# JSP-000116 Solution

## Problem Reference

JSP-000116

## Infinite Family of Minimum-Diameter Configurations

## Construction

Consider a configuration of four points:

$$
X=\{A,B,C,P\}
$$

Let \(A,B,C\) form an equilateral triangle with side length:

$$
|AB|=|BC|=|CA|=1
$$

The fourth point \(P\) satisfies the constraints:

$$
|PA|\le1
$$

$$
|PB|\le1
$$

$$
|PC|\le1
$$

The diameter of the configuration is defined as:

$$
D(X)=\max_{U,V\in X}|UV|
$$

---

## Minimum Diameter

Because the configuration contains the fixed edge:

$$
|AB|=1
$$

every feasible configuration satisfies:

$$
D(X)\ge1
$$

Therefore:

$$
D_{\min}\ge1
$$

Now consider the feasible region:

$$
R=B(A,1)\cap B(B,1)\cap B(C,1)
$$

Choose:

$$
P\in R
$$

Then:

$$
|PA|,|PB|,|PC|\le1
$$

Together with:

$$
|AB|=|BC|=|CA|=1
$$

all pairwise distances between the four points are at most 1.

Hence:

$$
D(X)\le1
$$

Combining the lower and upper bounds gives:

$$
D_{\min}=1
$$

---

## Infinite Noncongruent Family

Let \(G\) be the centroid of the equilateral triangle \(ABC\).

The distances from the centroid satisfy:

$$
GA=GB=GC=\frac{\sqrt3}{3}<1
$$

Therefore \(G\) lies inside the feasible region:

$$
B(A,1)\cap B(B,1)\cap B(C,1)
$$

and this region contains an open neighborhood around \(G\).

The fourth point \(P\) can therefore move continuously inside this neighborhood while preserving:

$$
D(X)=D_{\min}=1
$$

The triangle \(ABC\) acts as an active diameter core fixing the minimum diameter, while the point \(P\) provides a continuous degree of freedom.

Choose a continuous path:

$$
P(t)
$$

inside the feasible region such that:

$$
|P(t)A|
$$

varies with \(t\).

For two different parameters \(t_1,t_2\):

$$
|P(t_1)A|\ne |P(t_2)A|
$$

Therefore the two configurations cannot be congruent, because Euclidean congruence preserves all pairwise distances.

Hence:

$$
X(t_1)\not\cong X(t_2)
$$

and the family contains infinitely many pairwise noncongruent configurations.

---

## Conclusion

The distance-constrained system admits infinitely many noncongruent configurations attaining the minimum possible diameter.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{|\mathcal{M}|=\infty}
$$

---

## Method Summary

This construction demonstrates the following mechanism:

A rigid active core fixes the optimal value of the objective function, while a remaining continuous degree of freedom creates a positive-dimensional extremal configuration space.

In this case:

- The triangle \(ABC\) fixes the minimum diameter.
- The point \(P\) moves continuously within the feasible region.
- Distance invariants distinguish noncongruent configurations.

Therefore the set of minimum-diameter configurations is infinite.
