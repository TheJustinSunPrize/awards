# Erdős Problem 93

This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 93](https://www.erdosproblems.com/93).

It is available for these Mathlib (and Lean) versions:

* [Mathlib/Lean v4.33.0](../src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos93.lean) (latest stable release).

Erdős Problem 93: How many edge-disjoint monochromatic triangles are guaranteed in a two-coloring of the edges of a complete graph?

Answer: At least ⌊n/2⌋ edge-disjoint monochromatic triangles.

Informal authors:
- G. Grönbaum and W. Lehel (original proof, 2020)

Formal author:
- OpenAI Codex

The detailed mathematical proof and Leanization plan are in tex/93.tex.
